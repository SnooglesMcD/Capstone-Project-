This is the Capstone project entitled "Heart of the Home" made for the UCI GDIM Capstone class. 

The game follows a young man coming back to settle his fathers afairs after the death of both of his parents. The home still has the eclectic security measures in place from his gothic-loving father. Only he has the knowledge to best get through the house and find the papers he needs to settle his family's affairs and move on from his past. But lurking in the halls amongst the puzzles and dusty memories just might be the answers to the family secrets kept from him long ago. 

"Heart of the Home" is a narrative puzzle game made via Unity
